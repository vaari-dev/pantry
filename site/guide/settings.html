A few settings decide how much Pantry may spend and when it orders. Everyone can see them; only the owner can change them.

See your settings

Send /settings:

Delivery: Home: 12 Park Street (/setup to change) Daily limit: {{daily_cap}}, ₹189 spent today (/limit 1500) Smallest order: {{min_order}} (/minimum 99) Placing orders: on (/ordering on or off)

Daily limit

The most Pantry may spend in a day, across the whole family. It starts at {{daily_cap}}.

  • Change it with /limit 1500, or say “limit 2000 kar do”.
  • An order that would cross it isn’t placed; the basket waits, and Pantry tells you how much is left.
  • Orders Pantry couldn’t confirm count towards it too, to be safe.

Smallest order

Pantry waits until the basket reaches this amount before ordering, so you don’t pay Zepto’s fee for a small order. It starts at {{min_order}}.

  • Change it with /minimum 99, or say “minimum 150 kar do”.
  • Saying “abhi” or tapping Order now orders anyway. See Order right away.

Ordering on or off

With ordering off, Pantry does everything except pay: it puts your request into your Zepto cart, checks the bill, and stops. There’s no wait first.

🛒 Cart ready (₹189); placing orders is off (/settings).

Then open the Zepto app and check out yourself. It’s a good way to try Pantry, or to look over an unusual order first.

  • /ordering off and /ordering on switch it, as does saying “ordering band karo” or “ordering on karo”.
  • Each request replaces your Zepto cart with just that request, so check out in the app before sending the next.
  • Turning ordering back on empties Pantry’s basket, since those items went to your Zepto cart.

Address and login

The owner sends /setup to change the home address or reconnect Zepto. It runs the same three steps as the first time; your picks and history stay. See Connect your Zepto account.
