Pantry in one minute

  • You say it, by voice or text, in English or Hinglish. Pantry shows what it heard.
  • It learns your products. The first time you ask for something, you pick it from photos. After that, it just goes in.
  • It waits until there’s enough for one delivery, then gives you {{hold}} to add anything else.
  • It checks Zepto’s bill against your list before paying, from your Zepto wallet or in cash, within the limit you set.
  • Your family can join in, each from their own phone, into the same basket.
