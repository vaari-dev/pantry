Orders go to the address you chose when connecting, and are paid from your Zepto wallet or in cash at the door. Never by card.

Your delivery address

When you connect, you pick one of the addresses saved in your Zepto app. Every order goes there unless you say otherwise. /settings shows it.

To change it for good, the account’s owner sends /setup again and picks another saved address. To add a new address, save it in the Zepto app first.

Another address, for one order

Name another saved address in the message, by the label it has in your Zepto app:

2 milk, bread office pe bhej do
  • It applies while there’s anything in the basket, including items left over because they couldn’t be added. Once the basket is empty, orders go to your usual address again.
  • The basket shows it (“Delivering to Office”), and the ✅ says it: “✅ Ordered to Office, …”.
  • Anyone in the family can do this; only saved addresses work.
  • If the name matches none, or more than one, Pantry lists your saved addresses and changes nothing: Which saved address: Home, Office? Nothing changed; send it again naming one.

How you pay

Pantry pays one of two ways, decided by Zepto’s bill:

  • From your Zepto wallet, when the wallet covers the whole bill.
  • Cash on delivery, otherwise. You pay at the door as usual.

Pantry never pays by card or UPI.

Your daily limit

Pantry won’t place an order that would take the day’s spending past your limit ({{daily_cap}} to start). The owner can change it; see Settings and limits.

The day’s spending includes every order Pantry placed that day, and any it couldn’t confirm, to be safe. Orders you place yourself in the Zepto app don’t count.
