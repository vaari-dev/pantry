Why Pantry

The grocery run, minus everything you don’t miss.

No app to open, no aisles to scroll, no hunting for the brand you always buy. Just say what’s running out.

⚡

Seconds, not scrolling

🛡️

Your money, on a short leash

Questions

Fair questions, straight answers.

Does it cost anything?

Pantry is free. You pay Zepto for your groceries as you always would, from your Zepto wallet or in cash at the door.

What do I need?

Telegram, and a Zepto account with your delivery address saved in the Zepto app. Connecting takes about a minute.

What if it mishears me?

It shows you exactly what it heard and what’s in the basket before ordering, and gives you {{hold}} to fix it. Ask for far more than usual, and it checks with you first.

Can my family use it too?

Yes. Send an invite link from Pantry; everyone orders into the same basket, within the limits you set.

Can I try it without ordering?

Yes. Turn ordering off and Pantry fills your Zepto cart instead; you check out in the app when you’re ready.
