How it works

Three steps. You do the first one.

Talk the way you talk at home. The bot learns your brands once, then gets out of the way.

1

Say it

A voice note or a quick text, in English, Hinglish or a mix. Numbers too: do anda is two eggs, teen kela three bananas.

Changed your mind? Say that too: “bread hata do”, “make it 3 milk”.

🎙️ “ek doodh, dhania aur aloo”

2

Pick once

Something new? You see the shop’s options with photos, sizes and prices. Tap ✅ Pick and it’s remembered; next time it just goes in. No catalogue to scroll.

✅ Pick · Toned Milk (500 ml) · ₹27

3

It’s ordered

Once the basket reaches your minimum order (₹100 to start), the bot waits three minutes for anything you forgot, then orders. Or tap Order now, or just say “abhi”. Smaller lists wait in the basket, so a few items don’t cost a delivery fee.

✅ Ordered, ₹189 from the wallet

Safety

Nothing is ordered until Zepto’s own bill is right.

Before every order the bot sets the cart to exactly your basket, asks Zepto for the bill, and reads it back. One wrong line and it stops.

  1. Every item you asked forEach one is on the bill, matched by Zepto’s product id, not by name.
  2. At the quantity you asked for“2 in the cart, expected 3” stops the order.
  3. Nothing it didn’t addA stray item in the cart blocks the order. The cart belongs to your basket.
  4. Your saved addressThe bill must be for your address, or the saved one you named for this order.
  5. Deliverable right nowIf Zepto can’t deliver there, nothing is placed.
  6. Within today’s limit₹1,000 a day unless the owner says otherwise. Orders whose outcome is unclear count too.
  7. A total it can readNo clear amount to pay, no order.
All seven passThe order is placed: fully from the Zepto wallet, or fully cash on delivery. Never a card, never UPI.
Any one fails❌ Not ordered. You’re told why; the basket is kept.

And before the bill, more guards

👂
A mishearing can’t run up the bill

Far more than you usually buy? It asks first: ❓ egg ×20? You usually get ×2. And never more than 12 packs of one size.

⏳
Three minutes to change your mind

The basket and its total are shown before anything is ordered. Send more to add, or /clear to stop. Only “now” or “abhi” skips the wait.

🔘
Old buttons go dead

Only the latest Order now works, so an old message can’t cut a newer wait short.

📦
Honest about stock

Sold out? If you have another saved pick, it asks before swapping; if not, that item stays in the basket with the reason. Zepto caps a quantity? You’re told.

🛒
Cart-only mode

/ordering off fills the cart and stops. You check out in the Zepto app. Either way, the cart is the bot’s: each order replaces what was in it.

Reliability

Never twice. Never silently.

Paying is the one step that can’t be undone, so it is the one step built never to repeat. An order counts only when Zepto’s own history shows it.

📝

1 · Write it down

The attempt is recorded before the order call, and the basket cleared.

📤

2 · Place it

One call to Zepto, wallet or cash on delivery as the bill said.

🔎

3 · Find it

The returned order id must appear in Zepto’s order history.

✅

4 · Tell you

Only then: ✅ Ordered, with the amount and how it was paid.

⚡

Power cut between steps 1 and 3? On restart the bot finds the unfinished attempt and tells the owner. It does not try again, because the first try may have gone through.

🧵

One order at a time

A single worker runs every order in turn. Two family members tapping at once can’t produce two carts.

🔄

Updates wait for you

A restart lets the order in progress finish, with up to ten minutes’ grace; anything still queued, you’re asked to resend. A held basket resumes on its timer; more than five minutes late, it asks instead of ordering.

🩺

Checked every morning

At 9:00 the bot checks each account and renews its login days before it lapses. If something is off, the owner hears first.

💾

Crash-safe files

Every write lands whole or not at all: a new file, then an atomic swap. No half-written basket.

🧪

Tested before it ships

Every change passes lint, the test suite, a workflow check and a secret scan. Deploys run one at a time, are never cancelled halfway, and finish with a self-check.

🗣️

Errors in plain words

“The shop can’t deliver there right now.” Never a stack trace. If unsure, it says so: “Nothing was ordered unless you got a ✅.”

Security

Through the front door. Locked behind you.

Pantry talks to Zepto only through Zepto’s official connection for assistants. No scraping, no headless browser, no password to hand over.

Signing in

  • Zepto’s own OAuth sign-in with PKCE: your phone, then the code Zepto texts you. The bot never sees a password, because there isn’t one.
  • Your number and code vanish from the chat as soon as they are read; the bot repeats only the last four digits back.
  • Logins renew themselves days before they lapse. If Zepto ever refuses, the owner is told and sends /setup.

Who can order

  • Invite-only. Strangers who message the bot wait until the admin lets them in.
  • Family links work once, for 24 hours. Members can be removed at any time.
  • Only the owner changes the home address, the daily limit, the minimum order or the login. Anyone in the family can name another saved address for one order.
  • An owner who leaves takes the login with them. The next member inherits the account, not the keys.

The server

  • Nothing to knock on. The bot fetches its messages from Telegram and listens on no port, so nothing on the internet can call into it.
  • A locked-down container: runs as an unprivileged user, every Linux capability dropped, privilege escalation off.
  • Login tokens in owner-only files (mode 600), on one server, in one volume.

The code

  • Docker’s build actions are pinned to exact commits, not moving tags.
  • Secrets live in a GitHub deployment environment, written to the server owner-only, never into the image.
  • Every change is scanned for +91 phone numbers, bot tokens and login files; a hit fails the build.

Privacy

Your recording never leaves the server.

Speech becomes text on the bot’s own machine, with Whisper running offline, and the recording is deleted the moment it’s transcribed. To understand what you asked for, the text, typed or transcribed, goes to Anthropic’s Claude with your item names and basket. Nothing else does.

Logs record events, not conversations

The bot logs what happened, not what you said: no message text, no tokens, no addresses. Item names appear only when something goes wrong. A real line, ids changed:

Questions

Fair questions, straight answers.

Does it cost anything?

No fee from Pantry. You pay what Zepto’s bill says, from your Zepto wallet or in cash at the door.

Which shops does it work with?

Zepto, through its official connection for assistants. You need a Zepto account with a delivery address saved in the app.

What if it mishears me?

It replies with exactly what it heard and shows the basket with prices before ordering. You have three minutes to add more or send /clear, and an amount far above your usual is asked about first.

Can it spend more than I want?

It is built not to. The owner sets a daily limit (₹1,000 to start), and an order whose bill would cross it is refused. It pays only from the Zepto wallet or by cash on delivery, never a card or UPI.

What if an item is out of stock?

If you have another saved pick for it, the bot asks before swapping. If not, that item stays in the basket and you’re told why; the rest is ordered.

Could it ever order the same thing twice?

It is built not to. Every attempt is written down before the order call and confirmed by its id in Zepto’s history. An attempt that can’t be confirmed is reported to you and never retried.

Will it touch my Zepto app’s cart?

Yes. The cart is the bot’s while it orders: it is set to exactly your basket, and whatever was in it before is removed. Keep the app’s cart for the bot, or order from the app between bot orders.

I want to try it without it ordering.

Send /ordering off. The bot fills your Zepto cart and checks the bill, then stops; you finish in the app. /ordering on when you’re ready.

Does an AI read my messages?

Yes. Claude, by Anthropic, reads the text of each message to work out the items, edits and settings in it. Your recording never leaves the server, and the bot’s own code checks every item against your basket and Zepto’s bill before anything is ordered.

Can my family use it?

Yes, that’s the point. /invite gives you a link to forward to one person. They order from your account and wallet, within your limits. They don’t need /setup; sending it gives them an account of their own.

How do I delete my data?

/leave. If you’re the last one on the account, its login, picks and order history are deleted from the server.

Why “Pantry”?

Because it keeps yours stocked. Say what’s running low, and it’s on its way.
